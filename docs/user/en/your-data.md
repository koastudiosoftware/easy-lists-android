# Where your data lives

## On your device

Your lists, items, categories, tags, and photos are stored on your phone, inside the app's private storage. Other apps can't read them.

## What survives what

| What happens | Your lists, tags, categories and photos | Your settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You close the app or restart your phone | Kept | Kept |
| You update the app | Kept | Kept |
| You delete an item or list | Gone after the short Undo window, apart from your backups made earlier | Not affected |
| You uninstall the app | Removed | May come back through Google's automatic app backup |
| You reset or replace your phone | Removed, unless restored from a backup file | May come back through Google's automatic app backup |

## Keeping your data safe

Make a [local backup](local-backups.md) and store it somewhere that survives your phone, such as a folder synced to cloud storage. It's the only way your lists and photos come back after an uninstall or a reset.

Google's automatic app backup is deliberately limited to your settings. It doesn't store your lists or photos.
