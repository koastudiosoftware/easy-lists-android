# Local backups

## What's in a backup

You choose what to include:

- Settings
- Lists and list items (including your list order)
- Categories
- Tags (and which items they're on)
- Photos

Items you've deleted aren't included. Lists that other people shared with you aren't included either, because they belong to their owners.

## Where backups go

You pick a folder once. Each backup is a single file named `easy-lists-backup-` followed by the date and time.

The app doesn't limit how many backups there are, and it never deletes any, so you can remove old ones yourself. If the app loses access to the folder, it asks you to pick one again.

## Restoring

Choose a backup from the list, or pick a backup file from anywhere on your device. The app shows what's inside and asks you to confirm.

Restoring replaces your current data for the parts the backup contains, so anything you added after the backup was made is removed. Lists shared with you aren't affected. The change appears right away, with no restart.

## Reinstalling or switching phones

Google's automatic app backup doesn't save your lists or photos, only your settings. To keep your data safe through an uninstall or a reset, store your backups in a folder that survives it, such as one synced to cloud storage, or copy the backup files somewhere else.

After reinstalling, open Local backups and restore from the folder, or pick the backup file.

## Good to know

- Backups aren't encrypted, so anyone who has the file can read your lists and see your photos. Keep them somewhere private.
- A backup made by a newer version of the app can't be restored on an older one. Update the app first.
